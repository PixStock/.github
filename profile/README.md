### What it is

PixStock turns an old phone in airplane mode into a hardware-wallet-grade signer for tokenized stocks. The phone never goes online: orders cross the room as QR codes, and the vault checks the price against a signed quote before it signs.

- **Proven on mainnet:** a real swap (0.5 USDC into TSLAx) signed by a phone in airplane mode, from a vault that has never held a lamport.
- **Open spec and threat model** for the offline price check.
- **Two-minute demo** and a reproducible walkthrough in the repository.

👉 **[Read the code, the spec and the threat model](https://github.com/PixStock/pixstock)**
